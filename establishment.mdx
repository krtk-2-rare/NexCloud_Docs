### The Beginning

NexCloud was founded on **28 January 2026** with a simple goal to build reliable, affordable, and customer-focused hosting infrastructure for developers and communities.

What started as a small hosting initiative has grown into a platform focused on providing reliable infrastructure, modern management tools, and accessible hosting solutions.

NexCloud is **registered under the MSME (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises) framework** in India. This registration reflects our commitment to operating NexCloud as a structured and legitimate business while continuing to develop reliable hosting services for our customers.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to provide dependable hosting without unnecessary complexity. We aim to continuously improve our infrastructure, expand our services, and provide customers with a reliable platform they can build on.

From free hosting for new projects to managed and premium infrastructure for established services, NexCloud continues to grow with its community.
