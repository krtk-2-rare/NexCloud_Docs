> Free Hosting users can reset their server account password directly through the NexCloud Discord bot.

### Reset Your Password

- Join the official NexCloud Discord server.
- Open a channel where NexCloud bot commands are allowed.
- Send the following command:

```text
nc!user password
```

- The NexCloud bot will process your request.
- Your new password will be sent to you **privately via Discord DM**.

### If You Don't Receive the DM

Make sure:

- Your Discord DMs are enabled.
- You are using the correct NexCloud Discord server.
- You entered the command correctly.
- The bot is able to send you direct messages.

If the bot cannot DM you, contact NexCloud Support.

> **Security:** Never share the password sent to you by the bot with anyone. NexCloud staff will never ask you to provide your password.
