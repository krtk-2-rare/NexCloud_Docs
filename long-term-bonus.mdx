> NexCloud customers can receive **additional service duration at no extra cost** when purchasing eligible hosting plans for an extended billing period.

### Offer Benefits<br />

#### 3-Month Purchase

Purchase an eligible service for **3 months** and receive an additional **10 days of service FREE**.

> **Total Service Duration:** 3 Months \+ 10 Days

#### 5-Month Purchase

Purchase an eligible service for **5 months** and receive an additional **1 month of service FREE**.

> **Total Service Duration:** 6 Months

### Eligibility

This offer is available on eligible NexCloud hosting services purchased for the required duration.

### Excluded Plans

The following services are **not eligible** for this offer

- **GR Starter Plan**
- **All VPS Plans**

### Terms & Conditions

- The complete 3-month or 5-month service period must be purchased together.
- The bonus duration will be added to the service after the qualifying purchase is confirmed.
- The additional service period has **no extra charge**.
- Bonus days or months cannot be exchanged for cash, account credit, or refunds.
- The offer cannot be transferred to another account or service.
- Cancelled or refunded orders will not qualify for the bonus duration.
- NexCloud reserves the right to modify, extend, or discontinue this offer when necessary.
