Free Hosting gives you access to a simple hosting environment where you can deploy and manage your applications without purchasing a paid plan.

- Free server hosting
- Easy-to-use management panel
- Application console
- File management
- Resource management
- Server controls
- Community support

## Who Is It For ?

- Beginners learning to host applications
- Students and developers experimenting with projects
- Discord bot developers
- Small personal projects
- Testing and development environments
